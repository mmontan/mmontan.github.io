About

I'm a Software Engineer and Tech Lead at Google, focusing on Cloud AI Security. My work centers on agent security and model infrastructure security, ensuring that AI systems are built with robust security foundations.

My background combines deep expertise in distributed systems and security, with a PhD from the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign where I researched security monitoring systems for large-scale distributed infrastructure.
